PATH25 circuit component estimates
==================================

Use the circuit and rates of the PATH25 circuit reference. Increase the component cutoff through k = 8, record each interval and identify the first cutoff meeting the 10% target. At k = 8 all eight noisy locations are included.

The reference output is in ESTIMACION_H6_U3.json and RESULTADO_H6_U3.json.

The original protocol and report are archived together (12 November 2026).
