PATH25 runtime measurements and cancellation control
====================================================

Compare timing and memory for fixed batches of rates on the same instance. The resource driver records 28 runs. A separate check reproduces the weight-four cancellation under independent data and readout noise.

The reference output is in MEDICIONES_H6_U5.json and CONTROL_V14_FENOMENOLOGICO.json.
